# Cover document template

The cover is the first thing the client reads. It does three jobs: orients the reader, surfaces the audit's most important findings, and points to the right artefact for each question.

## Template

```
# <Project> · Persona research bundle

**Client:** <client name>
**Delivered:** <date>
**By:** <team>
**Status:** v<N> — see "Known limitations" below

---

## Executive summary

Three to five sentences. What the research found. How many archetypes, with role designation. The single most consequential finding.

Example (NV v1):
> Thirteen Stockholm consumers were interviewed about sustainable food. We
> built five archetypes: two primary design targets (Anna, the Caregiver Under
> Load; and Maja, the Constrained Aspirer), one supplemental calibration
> persona (Linn, the Integrated Practitioner), one negative persona (Bengt,
> the Continuity Keeper, who the strategy should design *around* rather than
> *for*), and one outlier note (IP03, the Principled Non-Participant, reachable
> only through a separate policy-debate channel). The strongest finding across
> the sample is that the audience has hit a wall on awareness content — every
> segment rejects it; the design lever is friction-reduction at the moment of
> choice, not more communication.

---

## What's in this bundle

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| 00 — Cover | This document |
| 01 — Themes | Six themes from the inductive thematic analysis |
| 02 — Framework | The seven behavioural variables we plotted against |
| 03 — Participant plot | The visual map of all participants on all variables |
| 04 — Archetypes | The five archetypes in text form |
| 05 — Persona cards (PDF) | The designed A3 cards for print and presentation |
| 06 — Persona cards (HTML) | The same, for digital sharing / further iteration |
| 07 — Validity audit | Our internal audit of the archetypes against the source |

---

## How to read it

- **If you have 10 minutes:** read this cover, then flip through the five persona cards (05).
- **If you have an hour:** read this cover, the persona cards (05), and the executive summary of the validity audit (07).
- **If you're handing this to your strategy / comms team:** all of the above, plus the themes (01) which give the behavioural grounding for messaging decisions.
- **If you're handing this to your design / service team:** all of the above, plus the framework (02) and the participant plot (03), which expose the design variables behind each persona.

---

## Known limitations

The validity audit (07) is the place to look for full detail. The three caveats worth raising upfront:

1. **<First limitation from the audit's executive summary>** — one sentence.
2. **<Second limitation>** — one sentence.
3. **<Third limitation>** — one sentence.

Example (NV v1):
> 1. Maja's composite spans three meaningfully different psychologies (class
>    resentment, self-regulation, postpartum bandwidth). Designing one message
>    for "Maja" will misread at least one of the three — consider sub-variants.
> 2. The archetypes describe failure mechanisms well and success mechanisms not
>    at all. The strategy work that follows should add a success-pathway layer.
> 3. The thirteen interviews are not statistically representative of Stockholm.
>    Population estimates of "how prevalent is each archetype" require a
>    follow-on quantitative study.

---

## Next steps

What the client could do with this. Not exhaustive — three or four options the team has talked about:

- Workshop with the strategy team to translate the archetypes into comms tone decisions
- Quantitative follow-on to size each archetype in the Stockholm population
- Channel-level work for the IP03 outlier (separate policy-publication track)

---

*Questions: <contact>*
```

## Tone notes

- Plain Swedish (or whatever the bundle language is). No jargon the client hasn't heard before.
- Don't oversell. The audit's job is to tell the client what's wrong; the cover's job is to point at the audit, not paper over it.
- One page if possible. Two if the audit findings need more room.
