# Seven validity checks — one-page reference

| # | Check | Question it answers | Operationalisation | Output verdict scheme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traceability | Does every claim in the archetype trace to verbatim source material? | For each claim, search composite members' interviews for at least one supporting quote. | GROUNDED · THIN · OVER-REACH · UNGROUNDED |
| 2 | Verbatim quote integrity | Are the quoted lines really in the source as quoted? | Word-by-word check of every attributed quote against the source interview. | VERIFIED · MILDLY EDITED · MINOR PARAPHRASE · FABRICATED |
| 3 | Negative case analysis | Does the source contain evidence that contradicts the archetype's defining traits? | Actively search composite members' interviews for opposing evidence; quote it with interview-id. | Findings list (severity assessed) |
| 4 | Composite coherence | Does the archetype acknowledge or smooth over meaningful variance within its composite? | For each composite, name the most important psychological / behavioural variance; check whether the archetype carries it through. | TIGHT · MIXED · SMOOTHED |
| 5 | Coverage / orphans | What material in the data does no archetype represent? | Per-participant: is the assigned archetype a good fit? Material no archetype carries? | Per-participant verdicts + sample-wide orphan list |
| 6 | Distinctiveness | Are archetypes that look similar meaningfully different in the source? | Pairwise check of archetypes at risk of collapsing; name claimed differences; verify against source. | REAL · BLURRED · OVERLAPPING |
| 7 | Triangulation + saturation | Are theme anchors accurate? Are themes uncovered? Is the sample saturated? | Cross-check archetype theme anchors against themes.md; flag uncovered themes; read last 2–3 interviews for emerging material. | Findings inline |

## Severity scheme for recommendations

- **HIGH** — directly undermines the validity of an archetype or the set
- **MEDIUM** — important but the archetype still holds; consider before client delivery
- **LOW** — polish; matters for audit trail more than for the artefact itself

## The single most important check

If you only have time for one: **traceability**. Every other check is a refinement of the central question — does the archetype say things the source supports?
